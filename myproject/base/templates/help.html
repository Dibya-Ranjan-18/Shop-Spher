❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How do I place an order?
Browse products on the home page, click "Add to Cart", then go to your Cart and click "Proceed to Checkout".
Can I change the quantity of items in my cart?
Yes. In the Cart page, use the "+" and "−" buttons next to each product to increase or decrease the quantity.
How do I remove an item from the cart?
Click the "Remove" button next to the product in your Cart page. It will be removed immediately.
Is my personal information safe?
Yes. We store only the information required for your account. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
Do I need an account to browse products?
No. You can browse all products without an account. However, you need to log in to add items to your cart.
